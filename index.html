Griffin's 6-player college football regular-season pick league. Claude runs the desk: picking order, watch cards, and payouts.

College Football · Regular Season 6 Players $60 Pot / Week Losers Split the Pot
How this works (commissioner rules)
  1. Roster — set the 6 player names once, edit anytime.
  2. Week 1 picking order is randomized. Every week after, order is: winners first (in their prior order), then losers (in their prior order).
  3. Each player picks one spread or moneyline per week, entered in their slot.
  4. The Parlay Watch Card auto-builds from the entered picks, in picking order.
  5. After games finish, mark each player Win / Loss / Push. The pot is $60, divided evenly among that week's losers — that's what each loser owes. Winners only get paid if it's a clean sweep — every single pick wins that week — in which case the $60 pot splits evenly among everyone. Any other result (a mix of wins/losses, or an all-loss week), winners get nothing.
  6. Season Stats tracks cumulative record and running balance for every player.
